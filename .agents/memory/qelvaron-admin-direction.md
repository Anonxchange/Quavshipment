---
name: Qelvaron admin direction
description: Product constraints for the Qelvaron operations dashboard and its later integrations.
---

The admin should extend the existing Webflow theme without changing the public pages. Supabase will provide backend/auth later, and Resend is the intended email provider after that connection.

The professional shipment printout should include full origin and destination locations, shipment details, and payment total, amount paid, balance, method, reference, and status. Do not invent unconfirmed Qelvaron contact details.

**Why:** The user requested this dashboard and named the intended backend and email-provider sequence.

**How to apply:** Keep access and email sending demo-only until Supabase and Resend are connected. Preserve the exported site branding and include all entered route and payment details on the printout.
