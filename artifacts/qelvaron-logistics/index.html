About us

A Logistics Partner That Thinks Like Your Business.

Qelvaron Global Logistics is based in Miami, Florida, and was built on a simple belief: moving goods should never be a source of uncertainty. Businesses depend on their freight arriving as planned, and we take that responsibility seriously. We work alongside importers, exporters, manufacturers and growing companies to organize freight by air, by sea and over land, shaping every solution around the cargo, the route and the timeline that matter most to you. Our team brings structure to complexity. We study each shipment before it moves, anticipate the points where delays tend to appear and coordinate every handover with care, so that the experience feels calm, transparent and dependable.

How It Works

Step 1

Share Your Requirements

Begin by telling us about your shipment, including the nature of your cargo, where it will be collected, where it must arrive and when. Our team reviews every detail with care so that the plan we build is accurate from the very first step.

Step 2

Receive a Tailored Proposal

We evaluate the available routes and modes of transport, then present a clear quotation with the options that suit your cargo, your budget and your schedule. You see the choices plainly and decide with complete confidence.

Step 3

We Coordinate Every Movement

Once you confirm, our team manages collection, documentation and transport from start to finish, keeping you informed at every important point. Your goods reach their destination handled with care and delivered as arranged, with our support available until the shipment is complete.

Supply Chain Coordination

We design logistics plans around the way your business actually operates, aligning transport, timing and handling so that goods move through your supply chain with greater visibility, fewer delays and more predictable outcomes. Every decision is made with the full journey in mind, from the point of origin to the final destination.

Distribution Planning

Reliable distribution depends on thoughtful preparation. We help you arrange delivery pathways that connect your goods with their destinations efficiently, reducing unnecessary handling, protecting your cargo and keeping your customers confident in every delivery you promise.

Route and Transport Planning

Each route carries its own demands. We weigh transit time, cargo requirements, cost and available transport options to recommend the path that serves your shipment best, so that speed and economy are balanced with the care your cargo deserves.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Contact Qelvaron

What logistics services does Qelvaron provide?

Qelvaron provides coordinated freight solutions by air, sea and land, supported by warehousing and distribution planning. Our team works with you to understand your cargo, your route and your timeline, then recommends the arrangement that suits your shipment best. Contact us with your requirements and we will confirm the options available for your destination.

How do I request a quotation?

Send us your cargo details, the collection and delivery locations and your preferred timeline through our quote request or by email. Our team will review your requirements carefully and respond with a clear proposal and the transport options available.

Can you manage urgent or high value shipments?

Yes. We give time sensitive and valuable cargo the extra planning it deserves, with attention to secure handling, suitable transport and clear communication throughout. Speak with our team early in the process so that we can design the most appropriate arrangement.

How will I receive updates on my shipment?

We keep you informed through the communication channels agreed at the time of booking, and our support team is always available to provide an update on request. Simply quote your shipment reference when you contact us.

How long will delivery take, and which destinations do you serve?

Transit times depend on the origin, the destination, the mode of transport and the documentation involved. Once we understand your shipment, we will provide a realistic estimate. We arrange freight to and from a wide range of destinations through our transport network and trusted partners, so tell us where your cargo needs to go and we will confirm availability for your route.
