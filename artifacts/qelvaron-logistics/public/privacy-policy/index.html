Website privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Website privacy notice. This page describes information handled by the Qelvaron Global Logistics website, not separate logistics-service arrangements. The registered legal entity, dedicated privacy contact, hosting details, retention periods, and governing jurisdiction are not stated on this page. This information is general and is not legal advice.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit the Qelvaron Global Logistics website. It is limited to this website and its current functions. If you arrange transportation, request a quote through a separate channel, or become a customer, additional notices and the applicable service documents may govern information used for that transaction.

1. Who is responsible

The website uses the public-facing name Qelvaron Global Logistics. The legal name of the entity that owns or operates the website has not yet been confirmed. In this policy, “Qelvaron,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the operator of this website under that public-facing name. Before publication as a final policy, the responsible legal entity and its principal business address should be stated here.

Qelvaron’s publicly stated business location is Miami, Florida, USA. This does not by itself establish the operator’s place of incorporation, the location of its servers, or the law that applies to a particular service.

2. Scope and services

This policy applies to information associated with visits to this website and the website’s current inquiry and tracking-page experience. It does not replace a customer contract, bill of lading, freight-forwarding agreement, carrier notice, employment notice, or other specific privacy notice that may apply to a separate relationship.

The public Tracking page can look up shipment records maintained in Supabase. Automated carrier feeds are not connected. The site does not provide user accounts or online payments. If other data uses or services are added, this policy should be reviewed and updated before that processing begins.

3. Information involved when you use this website

The categories below describe information that may be involved in the current website experience. The precise production configuration and provider retention practices have not yet been independently confirmed.

  • Information you choose to enter: for example, a name, email address, phone number, company name, or message if you type those details into an inquiry or newsletter form.
  • Tracking reference: if you submit a shipment reference in the Tracking search field, it is sent to the Supabase project configured for this website to look up the matching shipment record and customer-visible updates. The search does not create or change a shipment record.
  • Device and request information: a hosting or content-delivery provider may receive an IP address, browser and device attributes, requested page or asset, referral information, and request time when your browser loads the site.
  • Basic interaction information: your browser may keep technical data required to load or display pages and third-party resources. Browser storage behavior may vary by device and service provider.
  • Information you provide outside the site: if you contact Qelvaron through a separate channel or arrange a shipment, that information is handled under the relevant channel, agreement, and applicable notices rather than being collected by this static website.

Please do not submit payment-card details, government identifiers, precise location data, customs documents, health information, or other sensitive material through a general website field.

4. Website forms and the tracking page

Inquiry and newsletter forms

The inquiry and newsletter forms prepare entered details on the visitor’s device and may copy the inquiry to the device clipboard; they do not transmit those form contents to Qelvaron. Clipboard behavior is controlled in part by the browser and device. Do not treat a form’s on-screen confirmation as proof that Qelvaron received your message.

Shipment tracking

When you submit a reference, the Tracking page sends it to the Supabase project configured for this website to retrieve a limited shipment summary and customer-visible updates. The page does not request sender or recipient contact details, payment information, or internal notes, and it does not connect to automated carrier feeds. The lookup is read-only and does not create or edit shipment records.

5. How information may be used

Information handled through the website may be used to deliver pages and resources, maintain site availability, diagnose errors, protect the site against abuse, or let you prepare a message on your own device. Inquiry and newsletter forms do not send their contents to Qelvaron. A submitted tracking reference is processed by the configured Supabase project to perform the lookup described above.

If information is later received through a separate business channel, it may be used to respond to the request, evaluate or perform logistics services, communicate about a shipment, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect rights and property. The relevant service documents and a revised privacy notice should describe those activities.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, the responsible operator must identify the basis for the tracking lookup and other website processing. This notice does not select a jurisdiction or determine the applicable legal basis.

6. Service providers and sharing

The website loads design, font, script, and other page resources from third-party infrastructure, including Webflow-hosted content delivery and Google Fonts. The application also uses Supabase for shipment records and public tracking lookups. Providers may receive technical request information under their own terms and privacy notices. The Supabase project’s region, processing terms, and retention settings are controlled by that project’s configuration and are not listed on this page.

The production hosting provider controls its logging, security, and retention settings. The operator should keep this notice aligned with the active deployment, the providers that handle site data, and any analytics, advertising, or anti-abuse tools enabled for the website.

The inquiry and newsletter forms do not send their entries to Qelvaron. The tracking lookup sends a reference to Supabase as described above. Qelvaron may disclose information where required by law, to respond to valid legal process, to protect people or property, or as part of a business reorganization, subject to applicable law. Any other customer or shipment-data sharing should be described in the applicable service documents and privacy notice.

7. Retention

The tracking search sends the submitted reference to Supabase to perform a read-only lookup; the form does not create a separate search-history or shipment record. Shipment records and tracking updates remain in Supabase according to that project’s configuration. Retention and logging settings depend on the Supabase project and hosting providers. Information copied to a visitor’s clipboard remains under the visitor’s device and operating-system controls; Qelvaron cannot determine or control when the visitor’s device clears it.

Technical request logs, if created by the production host or content-delivery providers, are subject to those providers’ configuration and retention schedules. Information received through another business channel is handled under the relevant service arrangements and applicable legal obligations.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

The page source includes third-party fonts, scripts, and hosted assets. Their behavior and any browser storage they use may vary by provider and browser. The current source review did not identify a configured analytics or advertising product; that observation is not a guarantee that no provider uses cookies or similar technology.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies or scripts may affect page display or site functions. Any analytics, advertising, or non-essential tracking added to the website should be described here, with controls provided where required by law.

9. International processing

Qelvaron’s publicly stated location is Miami, Florida. Third-party hosting, font, and content-delivery infrastructure may process technical requests in other countries. Specific provider processing locations are not listed on this page. Where applicable law requires safeguards or disclosures for cross-border processing, the responsible operator must provide them.

10. Security

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Because the current site does not transmit the contents of its forms to Qelvaron, those fields should not be used for confidential or sensitive information. Security practices and controls vary by provider; review their published terms for provider-specific information.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the circumstances of the processing, applicable law may give you rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent; or to complain to a supervisory authority. Some rights are subject to exceptions and verification requirements.

The website forms do not deliver their contents to Qelvaron, so a message entered in those forms is not a request received by the operator. This website does not list a dedicated privacy email address or mailing address. Contact Qelvaron through your existing business channel to raise a privacy question or request.

12. Children

This website is intended for logistics customers and business contacts, not for children. If you believe a child has submitted information through this website, contact Qelvaron through your existing business channel.

13. Third-party links

This website may link to third-party websites. Qelvaron does not control those websites or their privacy practices. Review their notices before sharing information with them.

14. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when website features or data practices change. A dedicated privacy email address or mailing address is not listed on this page. Contact Qelvaron through your existing business channel to raise a privacy question or request. A message entered in the website form is not delivered to Qelvaron.
