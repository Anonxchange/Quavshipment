Website privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Draft for legal review. This notice is written for the public-facing website of Qelvaron Global Logistics. The precise registered legal entity, privacy contact, production hosting provider, retention periods, and governing jurisdiction have not been confirmed. Those details and the website’s cookie and processor configuration must be verified before this draft is treated as a final legal notice. This document provides general information and is not legal advice.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit the Qelvaron Global Logistics website. It is limited to this website and its current functions. If you arrange transportation, request a quote through a separate channel, or become a customer, additional notices and the applicable service documents may govern information used for that transaction.

1. Who is responsible

The website uses the public-facing name Qelvaron Global Logistics. The legal name of the entity that owns or operates the website has not yet been confirmed. In this policy, “Qelvaron,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the operator of this website under that public-facing name. Before publication as a final policy, the responsible legal entity and its principal business address should be stated here.

Qelvaron’s publicly stated business location is Miami, Florida, USA. This does not by itself establish the operator’s place of incorporation, the location of its servers, or the law that applies to a particular service.

2. Scope and services

This policy applies to information associated with visits to this website and the website’s current inquiry and tracking-page experience. It does not replace a customer contract, bill of lading, freight-forwarding agreement, carrier notice, employment notice, or other specific privacy notice that may apply to a separate relationship.

The current website does not provide user accounts, online payments, or a connected carrier-tracking service. If these functions are added, this policy should be reviewed and updated before the new collection or processing begins.

3. Information involved when you use this website

The categories below describe information that may be involved in the current website experience. The precise production configuration and provider retention practices have not yet been independently confirmed.

  • Information you choose to enter: for example, a name, email address, phone number, company name, or message if you type those details into an inquiry or newsletter form.
  • Tracking reference: if you enter a shipment reference in the Tracking search field, it is used only in your browser to display the current availability notice. The website does not send that reference to Qelvaron or a carrier.
  • Device and request information: a hosting or content-delivery provider may receive an IP address, browser and device attributes, requested page or asset, referral information, and request time when your browser loads the site.
  • Basic interaction information: your browser may keep technical data required to load or display pages and third-party resources. The site's cookie and local-storage behavior must be audited before this notice is final.
  • Information you provide outside the site: if you contact Qelvaron through a separate channel or arrange a shipment, that information is handled under the relevant channel, agreement, and applicable notices rather than being collected by this static website.

Please do not submit payment-card details, government identifiers, precise location data, customs documents, health information, or other sensitive material through a general website field.

4. Website forms and the tracking page

Inquiry and newsletter forms

As currently implemented, website forms prepare the entered details on the visitor’s device and may copy that draft to the device clipboard. They do not transmit the form contents to Qelvaron or submit them to a server. Clipboard behavior is controlled in part by the browser and device. Do not treat a form’s on-screen confirmation as proof that Qelvaron received your message.

Shipment tracking

The Tracking page offers a browser-only reference field. If you enter a reference, the page displays a notice that live tracking is not connected. The current implementation does not send the reference to Qelvaron or a carrier, query shipment systems, or store the value on a Qelvaron server. The reference remains in your browser field until cleared or the page is closed; browser-specific history or suggestion behavior may still apply. The message shown is not a tracking result. When an operational tracking service is introduced, this section must explain the information collected, the carrier or provider involved, and the applicable retention and sharing practices.

5. How information may be used

Information handled through the current website may be used to deliver the requested page and its resources, maintain site availability, diagnose errors, protect the site against abuse, or let you prepare a message on your own device. Qelvaron does not receive the text you enter into the current static forms through those forms.

If information is later received through a separate business channel, it may be used to respond to the request, evaluate or perform logistics services, communicate about a shipment, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect rights and property. The relevant service documents and a revised privacy notice should describe those activities.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, the operator will identify and document the appropriate basis for each purpose in the final policy. No legal basis is asserted here for data that the website does not currently transmit to Qelvaron.

6. Service providers and sharing

The website loads design, font, script, and other page resources from third-party infrastructure, including Webflow-hosted content delivery and Google Fonts. Shared content-delivery networks may also serve libraries or assets. When your browser requests those resources, the provider may receive technical request information such as your IP address, browser details, and the resource requested, under that provider’s own terms and privacy notices.

The production hosting provider and its logging, security, and retention settings have not been confirmed for this draft. Before publication, the operator should verify the deployed provider, any service providers with access to site data, relevant data-processing terms, and whether additional analytics, advertising, or anti-abuse tools are enabled.

The current site is not configured to sell website form entries; those forms do not send entries to Qelvaron. Qelvaron may disclose information where required by law, to respond to valid legal process, to protect people or property, or as part of a business reorganization, subject to applicable law. Any future customer or shipment-data sharing must be described in the applicable service documents and updated privacy notice.

7. Retention

The current inquiry forms do not transmit their contents to Qelvaron. A tracking reference entered in the search field remains in the active browser field and is not stored on a Qelvaron server; browser-specific history or suggestions may be controlled by your device. Information copied to a visitor’s clipboard remains under the visitor’s device and operating-system controls; Qelvaron cannot determine or control when the visitor’s device clears it.

Technical request logs, if created by the production host or content-delivery providers, are subject to those providers’ configuration and retention schedules. Those schedules have not been confirmed here. If Qelvaron receives information through another channel, it should retain that information only for as long as reasonably needed for the stated business purpose, legal obligations, accounting, dispute resolution, or enforcement of agreements, and then delete or de-identify it where appropriate.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

The page source includes third-party fonts, scripts, and hosted assets. Their behavior and any browser storage they use should be tested in the production build before this policy is finalized. The current source review did not identify a configured analytics or advertising product, but that observation is not a complete cookie audit and should not be read as a guarantee that no provider uses cookies or similar technology.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies or scripts may affect page display or site functions. If analytics, advertising, or non-essential tracking is later added, the operator should identify each tool, explain its purpose, obtain consent where required, and provide any legally required controls before that tool is enabled.

9. International processing

Qelvaron’s publicly stated location is Miami, Florida. Third-party hosting, font, and content-delivery infrastructure may process technical requests in other countries. The actual production providers and processing locations have not been confirmed for this draft. Where applicable law requires safeguards or disclosures for cross-border processing, the responsible operator should identify the relevant countries and safeguards before publication.

10. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect information handled by the website operator and its providers. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Because the current site does not transmit the contents of its forms to Qelvaron, those fields should not be used for confidential or sensitive information. Provider-specific security controls have not been independently verified for this draft.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the circumstances of the processing, applicable law may give you rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent; or to complain to a supervisory authority. Some rights are subject to exceptions and verification requirements.

The current static forms do not deliver their contents to Qelvaron, so a message entered in those forms is not a request received by the operator. A dedicated privacy-request email address and mailing address have not been confirmed. They must be supplied and monitored before this policy is used as a final notice. If Qelvaron has received your information through a separate channel, use the verified privacy contact provided through that channel.

12. Children

This business website is intended for companies and adults arranging or learning about logistics services; it is not directed to children under 18. Children should not submit personal information through the website. If the operator learns that a child has provided personal information directly through a service it controls, it should take appropriate steps under applicable law.

13. Third-party links

The website may link to social networks, map services, carriers, or other external websites. Following one of those links takes you to a service operated by another party. That party’s privacy policy and terms apply to your interaction there; Qelvaron does not control the privacy or security practices of external sites.

14. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when the website, services, legal requirements, or information practices change. The date at the top of the page indicates the stated revision date. Material changes should be explained here, and any notice or consent required by law should be provided before the revised practices begin.
